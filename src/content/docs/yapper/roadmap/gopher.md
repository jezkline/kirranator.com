*Planned*

An add-on for Yapper: a separate product that works on its own and plugs into Yapper's conversations when you have both. The name is a working title.

Plans can change, and there are no dates yet. If something here matters to your project, or you'd like something that isn't listed, email [contact@kirranator.com](mailto:contact@kirranator.com).

A quest system built to work with Yapper:

- Pick up and turn in quests through conversation, with nodes for giving, advancing and completing them.
- Tipsters: characters who point the player to quests and react to their progress.
- Objectives that track items picked up, delivered or turned in, using [Hoarder](/yapper/roadmap/hoarder/) or your own inventory.
- Quest conditions in the graph editor, so dialogue can respond to what the player has started, finished or failed.

Until then, the [quests recipe](/yapper/integration/#recipe-quests) shows how to connect your own quest system.
